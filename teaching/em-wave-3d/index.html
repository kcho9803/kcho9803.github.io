Electromagnetic wave in 3D

Explore how the electric and magnetic fields oscillate together as a plane wave travels through vacuum.

The electric and magnetic fields are perpendicular to the direction of travel.

Wave equations

Ey=Emax⁡cos⁡(2π(tT−xλ))E_y=E_{\max}\cos\left(2\pi\left(\frac{t}{T}\pm\frac{x}{\lambda}\right)\right)
Bz=Bmax⁡cos⁡(2π(tT−xλ))B_z=\mp B_{\max}\cos\left(2\pi\left(\frac{t}{T}\pm\frac{x}{\lambda}\right)\right)
Emax⁡=cBmax⁡ ,T=1fE_{\max}=cB_{\max}\,,\quad T=\frac{1}{f}

At the probe

x=0,tT=0.000,θ=0∘x=0,\quad \frac{t}{T}=0.000,\quad \theta=0^\circ
Ey=1.00cos⁡(0∘)=1.00 V/mE_y=1.00\cos(0^\circ)=1.00\,\mathrm{V/m}
Bz=3.34cos⁡(0∘)=3.34 nTB_z=3.34\cos(0^\circ)=3.34\,\mathrm{nT}

The minus sign in the phase gives propagation toward +x.

θ=2π(tT−xλ)\theta=2\pi\left(\frac{t}{T}\pm\frac{x}{\lambda}\right)
E⃗1=E1,max⁡cos⁡θ y^\vec E_1=E_{1,\max}\cos\theta\,\hat y
E⃗2=E2,max⁡cos⁡(θ−δ) z^\vec E_2=E_{2,\max}\cos(\theta-\delta)\,\hat z

At the probe

x=0,tT=0.000,θ=0∘x=0,\quad \frac{t}{T}=0.000,\quad \theta=0^\circ
E1,max⁡=1.00,E2,max⁡=1.00 V/mE_{1,\max}=1.00,\quad E_{2,\max}=1.00\,\mathrm{V/m}
δ=90∘\delta=90^\circ
E⃗=E⃗1+E⃗2=(1.00,0.00) V/m\vec E=\vec E_1+\vec E_2=(1.00,0.00)\,\mathrm{V/m}
cB⃗=sx^×E⃗=(0.00,1.00) V/mc\vec B=s\hat x\times\vec E=(0.00,1.00)\,\mathrm{V/m}

Components (y, z). The direction sign s is +1.

λ=550 nm,f≈545 THz\lambda=550\,\mathrm{nm},\quad f\approx545\,\mathrm{THz}
T=1.83 fs ,ω=2πTT=1.83\,\mathrm{fs}\,,\quad \omega=\frac{2\pi}{T}
ω=3.42×1015 rad/s\omega=3.42\times10^{15}\,\mathrm{rad/s}
