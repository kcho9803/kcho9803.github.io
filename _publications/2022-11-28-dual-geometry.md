## Overview

Entanglement entropy connects quantum information to geometry through holography. In this paper, we investigate the inverse problem: using entanglement data to recover a gravitational description of a quantum field theory.

We combine deep learning with the Ryu–Takayanagi relation to reconstruct the dual geometry. We then examine how this reconstructed geometry encodes additional physical information, including infrared thermodynamic properties.
