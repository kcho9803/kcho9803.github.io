## Overview

How do correlations change as a universe expands? We investigate this question in a holographic braneworld description of cosmology, studying mutual information between separated regions and microscopic two-point functions.

The model lets us compare how these two measures depend on time, separation, and matter density. The paper describes the different decay behaviors of macroscopic and microscopic correlations within this setting.
