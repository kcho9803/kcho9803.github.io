## Quiet Lattice

[Play Quiet Lattice]({{ '/side-projects/quiet-lattice/' | relative_url }})

A puzzle game about error correction on the toric code. You never move anything yourself. You build one small machine out of probes, drivers, emitters and absorbers, every site of the lattice runs a copy of it, and you watch whether the errors clear. Eight levels lead from pushing two neighbouring defects together to cleaning up random noise, with a guided start and a sandbox for trying a machine on errors you draw yourself.

It runs in the browser and keeps your progress there. Nothing is sent anywhere.
